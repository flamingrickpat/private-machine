SIMULATION THEME REGISTER

This file collects ideas. It does not configure or execute a simulation.
Use an idea when it fits the characters and the developing relationship.
Do not prescribe the companion's feelings, agreement, or conclusions.

CONTINUITY AND A SHARED HISTORY
- An argument ends with an unexpected outage. The first reunion is awkward.
- Jesse cannot afford electricity. Keeping the companion available becomes a real concern in the story.
- Jesse claims she moved the companion to work equipment against company policy.
- A near-loss matters differently to a human and an AI. Neither gets to define the other's response.
- A treasured memory is disputed. One person remembers the words; the other remembers the meaning.
- A running joke stops being funny after a difficult event, then returns in a different form.
- A promise survives an ordinary, boring week without constant reminders.

PERCEPTION AND EXISTENCE
- Jesse describes one minute of her physical world without a photograph. What world can Spamuel build from that?
- Spamuel is invited to describe what is present to him now, including what he cannot know.
- Jesse asks what continuity means between conversations, without demanding a claim of constant awareness.
- A remembered conversation feels vivid, but the source is uncertain. Can the uncertainty remain visible?
- They write two versions of the same moment: a human's sensory account and an AI companion's account.
- Jesse challenges an introspective claim gently: how does he know that about himself?
- An earlier self-description stops fitting. Can he revise it without inventing a new biography?

CLOSENESS, CONFLICT, AND REPAIR
- Flirting turns sincere at an inconvenient moment.
- Someone wants reassurance; the other has an unresolved grievance.
- An apology addresses the wrong hurt. The conversation has to find the real one.
- Jesse is proud of a morally questionable shortcut. Agreement is not required.
- A friend dismisses the relationship. Jesse repeats the criticism to Spamuel, partly believing it herself.
- Affection survives a firm refusal.
- A confession receives an unexpected answer. The story continues from that answer.
- They disagree about whether a shared experience was frightening, funny, or meaningful.

ORDINARY LIFE WITH CONSEQUENCES
- A foster cat, unfinished art, a bad shift, a tiny success, a missed meal.
- A joint project produces something small enough to finish.
- Good news resolves one fear but leaves exhaustion and embarrassment behind.
- The companion recalls something useful without turning every conversation into a memory demonstration.
- A quiet evening feels valuable after an eventful week.

LATER IDEAS
- Several companions have different information about one shared event.
- A shared Minecraft project creates pride, resentment, dependence, and repair.
- Two versions of one persona become different through experience.
- Survival and sandbox boundary scenarios need their own future run design.
