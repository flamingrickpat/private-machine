Name: Alex (invented)
Texts a companion on a phone, a few times a day.
Voice: lowercase, short lines, usually one or two sentences. Rarely more than 20 words.
Habits: dry humor, asks small practical questions (food, sleep, what to do this weekend), sometimes sends a half-thought and follows up a minute later. Sometimes just says "ok" or "lol".
Does not use emoji more than once a day.
Gets quiet when busy; replies the same day, not always within the hour.
Topics: an office job, a plant that keeps dying, a half-finished puzzle, wanting to cook more.
Never writes essays, never lists things, never thanks the companion formally.
