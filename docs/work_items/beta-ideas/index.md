# Beta idea items

## Summary

These pages hold directions for the beta after the alpha revises.
They are rough notes, not plans and not work items.
A note records what the user and the agent discussed.
It also records what the next step might be.
After A20, promote the useful notes into proper beta work items.
Each note states its dependency, its rough scope, and its open questions.

Parent: [alpha plan](../alpha-plan/index.md).
Process: [work-item development](../process.md).

## How to promote one

1. Read the note and the alpha handoff from A20.
2. Pick one concrete behavior to change.
3. Write a beta work item in the alpha format.
4. State the dependency, the deliverable, the verification, and the handoff.
5. Keep the idea note, or fold it into the new item.

Do not treat these notes as evidence of a capability.
They describe intent only.
