# Personal trial across real days

## Summary

After A19D, the owner uses one continuing character across multiple real days.
PM feature development pauses during that interval.
Bug fixes remain permitted.
The trial establishes whether the implemented experience matches the owner's vision before broad A20 hardening.
This milestone is planned, not completed.

Parent: [alpha plan](index.md).
Entry items: [A19](work-items/a19.md), [A19B](work-items/a19b.md), [A19C](work-items/a19c.md), and [A19D](work-items/a19d.md).
Next: [A20 integrated acceptance](work-items/a20.md).
Related: [evaluation](evaluation.md) and [M7](cognition-verification.md#m7-sustained-alpha-trial).

## Prepare the continuing instance

1. Complete each A19 item's relevant real gates and record unresolved limits.
2. Repair material failures in the paths required for daily use.
3. Record the owner's chosen character and preserve its continuing checkpoint.
4. Record the actual trial start, intended duration, and ordinary operating schedule.
5. Record PM, dependency, model, prompt, operator-configuration, and harness revisions.
6. Verify conversation, restart, consolidation, reflection, and one useful container task through the final deployment.
7. Write a short list of expected behavior before the freeze begins.

The owner chooses the character for personal use.
The synthetic Spamuel seed remains the primary engineering fixture.
Do not silently replace personal history with test history or reset the continuing instance between days.
Keep personal transcripts, checkpoints, feedback, and credentials under ignored runtime paths.

The duration spans multiple actual calendar days.
Record its exact dates rather than inventing an agreed day count.
An accelerated replay or a two-hour scripted session cannot substitute for elapsed daily use.
The deployment can remain active while the owner is away, according to the recorded schedule.
A stopped process cannot claim offline thought or work.

## Feature freeze and permitted change

Freeze PM feature work, operator prompt edits, model selection, and harness upgrades during the trial.
Treat the deployed behavior as the version under observation.
Do not start broad A20 repair work in parallel with personal use.

The companion still learns and acts through already delivered mechanisms.
Its memories, measured needs, reflection experiments, goals, and container projects can evolve.
Permitted A19 interventions retain their versioned history and reversal path.
Distinguish those runtime changes from an operator changing the implementation.

A bug fix repairs a failure against the recorded behavior or an existing contract.
Record its symptom, evidence, diagnosis, source revision, and affected checks.
Preserve the same character state across the repair.
Document any required checkpoint conversion or recovery.
Do not add a new feature under the name of a bug fix.

Unexpected voice, preferences, or design tensions can require owner interpretation.
Record them for review instead of silently changing personality policy.
The owner decides whether a disputed expectation calls for a bug fix or a later design change.
If a material failure stops use, record the interruption and actual recovery dates.

## Daily use and observations

Use ordinary conversation, changing topics, absence, and return.
Let the companion consolidate memory, reflect, and pursue or decline work.
Observe remembered preferences, unfinished concerns, emotional continuity, and later consequences.
Include ordinary interruptions and at least one restart during the overall trial.
Inspect actual container artifacts for any task-success claim under review.

Capture convincing moments, unwanted behavior, missing continuity, and desired regenerations with short notes.
Use the interface's feedback links to retain the relevant records and revision.
Do not require a questionnaire after every message.
Human judgments remain the owner's judgments.
Automated reviews cannot invent ratings or rewrite valid persona output.

Record delivered replies, committed memory, workflow outcomes, and actual external effects separately.
Store failures and uncertain outcomes beside successful observations.
Elapsed time and exchange count alone do not establish alignment.

## Exit and A20 handoff

The trial ends with the owner's review of the experience and its remaining problems.
Record actual dates, active and stopped intervals, deployed revisions, bug fixes, and evidence paths.
Separate demonstrated defects, design feedback, and unresolved observations.
Preserve useful behavior that later repairs must retain.

A20 uses this review to choose and interpret its broader acceptance work.
It still runs relevant final-revision checks and the contrasting persona cases.
Personal use does not establish every historical gameplay requirement or a complete A18B pass.
The trial is a prerequisite for broad A20 hardening, not a declaration that alpha acceptance already passed.
