# A12: Control and recover interrupted body work

## Summary

This item preserves control and evidence when body work is cancelled, disconnected, or interrupted.
The implementation and real interruption evidence are complete.
A13 remains pending and owns the separate gameplay-replanning milestone.

Parent: [ordered alpha plan](../index.md).
Dependencies: A06, A10, A11.
Design: [body ownership](../minecraft-operations.md#body-ownership-and-session-lifetime).

### Deliver

1. Preserve body ownership, instruction revisions, and execution epochs across worker lifecycle changes.
2. Reconcile interrupted world actions through fresh observation before resuming a leaf.
3. Recover accepted-but-unpublished intents and progress through durable receipts.

Use one mutation lease per body.
A checkpoint preserves intent and observations.
It cannot roll back external world effects.

### Start from

`runtime/managed_runs.py`, `runtime/steward.py`, Ghost checkpoints, and bus publication.
Inspect pm-workflows external state policy and pm-coder cancellation.
Extend current interrupted receipt behavior rather than silently restarting every old task.

The publication-recovery mechanism should cover companion intents as well as Minecraft work.
Replay must not blindly repeat an uncertain world mutation.
Public reply reconciliation uses identity and publication status rather than inventing a new turn.

### Verify

Interrupt before a call, during an in-flight call, after its effect, and before its receipt is published.
Disconnect the MCP body and restart the process.
Verify retained evidence, stale epoch rejection, and a fresh observation on resume.

Submit instruction changes and cancellation while a task is active.
No later action may use a superseded accepted instruction revision.
A cancelled world effect remains documented.
Two simultaneous resume attempts cannot both own the body.

### Manual test

Cancel, reconnect, and resume the wood task.
Restart during a house leaf.
Inspect what was retained and which observation justified the next action.

### Handoff

Supply lifecycle semantics, reconciliation rules, control commands, failure-injection evidence, and the remaining external-effect uncertainties.
