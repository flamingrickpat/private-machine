# A16: Deliver a grounded codelet repertoire

## Summary

This item delivers grounded codelet families whose interactions affect behavior.
It is not implemented by this plan.
It grows the A15 catalogue from eleven codelets into a real repertoire.
The repertoire must cover the full emotion range, not a cheerful subset.
It must also carry the named affect pathways from the old `pm_next` catalogue.
A later beta step can evolve the repertoire with the old codelet-discovery pipeline.

Parent: [ordered alpha plan](../index.md).
Dependencies: A15.
Design: [codelet lessons](../previous-attempts.md#codelets-and-internal-thought) and [codelets](../cognition.md#codelets-and-shared-attention).

### Deliver

1. Rewrite an initial repertoire of roughly 24–40 useful codelets across the old catalogue's ten families.
2. Add explicit pathways and persona-dependent activation without narrator or invented-history framing.
3. Demonstrate causal family interactions through real scenarios.
4. Cover the full emotion range, including anger, resentment, jealousy, disgust, contempt, boredom, and mischief.
5. Keep each codelet's `data_aquisation` checklist as context requirements.

The ten families cover appraisal, memory, imagination, valuation, identity monitoring, coping, needs, attention, action tendencies, and narratives.
The count is a delivery range, rather than a behavioral acceptance metric.
Add further codelets when a concrete missing behavior justifies them.

### Named pathways

The old catalogue has two useful layers:

- `pm_next/pm_core/subsystems/codelet/codelet_definitions.py` — 342 codelets in ten families.
- `pm_next/pm_core/subsystems/codelet/pathways_detailed.py` — 22 named pathways that chain codelets with activation weights.

The named pathways are:

`love, anxiety, curiosity, empathy_compassion, shame_self_worth, playfulness_creativity, persistence_goal_pursuit, greed, pride, envy, wrath, lust, sloth, gluttony, paranoia, ocd_loops, borderline, narcissism, machiavellian, schizotypal, hypomania, dissociation`.

These pathways are the design material for a full human emotion range.
They let a small repertoire produce complex, persona-dependent behavior.
Port the pathway mechanism before copying any clinical pathway whole.
Add one pathway only when a real scenario shows its distinct behavior.
Never present a clinical pathway as a diagnosis of a real person.

### Full emotion range

The repertoire must let the companion feel and act on negative and mixed states:

- Anger and resentment can drive a refusal, a confrontation, or a permitted destructive game action.
- Jealousy and envy can change a relationship choice.
- Disgust and contempt can change an affiliation choice.
- Boredom can drive exploration or mischief.
- Mixed feelings can hold two emotions at once.
- A dark mood can persist across cycles without a forced cheerful reset.

Do not require a cheerful conclusion to any scenario.

### Start from

- `pm_next/pm_core/subsystems/codelet/codelet_definitions.py` — 342 codelets, families, and `data_aquisation` checklists.
- `pm_next/pm_core/subsystems/codelet/pathways_detailed.py` — the 22 named pathways.
- `pm_next/pm_core/subsystems/codelet/codelet_percepts.py` — percept types, including the MIDCA and qualia percepts.
- `workflows/main_cognition/stages/codelets/catalogue.yaml` — the A15 catalogue to grow.
- `workflows/main_cognition/codelet_schedule.py` — the scheduler to extend with pathway boosts.

Rewrite prompts around current state, attributed memories, and capabilities.
Do not import old embodied prompts that invent senses or past events.

Include disappointment and repair, curiosity and exploration, shared achievement,
concern and recovery, self-doubt, and deliberate focus.
Imagination produces possibilities with explicit attribution.
Narrative integration cannot invent executed past events.

### Verify

Demonstrate several multi-family pathways with real generation.
A retrieved failure can influence simulation and then a changed method.
A shared success can influence affect, memory, and a later cooperative goal.
An appraised anger can reach a permitted action through the action-tendency family.
A jealousy or envy pathway can change a later relationship choice.

Verify mixed feelings, competing motives, and negative affect without a mandatory cheerful conclusion.
Codelets that never activate or never affect accepted behavior remain unproven.
Measure the selected subset and inference consumption per scenario.
Keep the fixed scheduler rules as the regression baseline for a later agentic replacement.

### Manual test

Replay a meaningful shared event with two trait profiles.
Inspect activated families and compare later choices.
Evaluate whether the difference feels causally consistent.
Run one scenario that makes the companion angry and inspect the resulting choice.

### Handoff

Supply the repertoire catalogue, activation evidence, pathway traces, persona
configuration, and documented inactive definitions.
Record which pathways stayed unused and why.
