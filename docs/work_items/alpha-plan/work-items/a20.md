## Sustained trial and fixtures

Complete at least two hours of mixed use for each selected persona.
Include conversation, idle periods, self-origin activity, changed goals, an expected failure, and restart.
Elapsed session time alone does not establish useful behavior.
Preserve per-exchange and per-episode evidence.

Choose modest tasks with independently observable outcomes.
An agent-backed note or a small game task can establish action integration.
Inspect actual file bytes or world effects.
A completed workflow receipt alone does not establish task success.

Large changed-house and diamond scenarios are not required in this revised companion trial.
Record historical M3 and M4 as passed, failed, or deferred according to their actual evidence.
A narrow task cannot establish repaired large gameplay.
Do not describe companion acceptance as full acceptance of every historical requirement.
