# A19F: Rebuild main cognition on a situational model and a sharded workspace

## Summary

Replace the main cognition cycle with a typed LIDA-style cycle.
A persistent current situational model (CSM) collects unconscious content.
Codelets fill the CSM, structure-building codelets bind its items, and attention codelets move structures into workspace shards.
Every model call in the cycle is an immersive story and choice simulator, not an assistant.
Action selection makes zero or one decision for each stimulus group, in character, and a separate routing step maps decisions to workflows.
This item is planned, not delivered.

Parent: [alpha plan](../index.md).
Dependencies: A15, A16, A17, A18C, and the first A19 slice.
Decisions: D22–D29 in the [decision register](../decision-register.md).
Sources: [curated cognitive sources](../cognitive-sources.md), the LIDA and MIDCA texts in the local research collection, and the old generation `C:/source/pm/private-machine-head`.
Next: [A19G inner loop on the shared cycle](a19g.md).

## Engineering rules

These rules apply to all A19F code.

- Validate each input once, at its entry, with `Model.model_validate`. A failed validation of our own data is a bug. Let it crash.
- Do not use `.get("…")`, `getattr(obj, "…", default)`, or `isinstance` probes on our own models. Every field exists and has a default. `x or 0.0` is acceptable for an optional value.
- Do not catch exceptions from our own code. Do not add fallback values for missing data.
- Retry only failures outside our control: provider transport errors and model output that fails validation. Retry invalid model output with a repair turn until it is valid. Log every attempt.
- Never put a persona name in code. Names come from `PersonaConfig` and entity records.
- Put tunable numbers in `pm/constants.py`. Do not scatter them in steps.
- Keep `pm/` free of `workflows/` imports. `tests/unit/test_architecture.py` checks this.
- Follow the [naming rules](../../../concepts/naming.md). Record classes end in `Record`.

| `csm` | `SituationalModel` | Unconscious content with activation. |

| `codelets` | `CodeletRegistryState` | Runtime state of every registered codelet. |

Delete `internal_data`. A19G gives the inner loop typed fields for its own data.

### Shard keys

`ShardKey` names a shard: `kind` is `self`, `entity`, or `workflow`, and `ref` is empty, an entity id, or a workflow name.
`GlobalWorkspace` holds `self_shard`, `entity_shards: dict[int, WorkspaceShard]`, and `workflow_shards: dict[str, WorkspaceShard]`.
A shard for an entity or workflow appears when its first stimulus arrives. It stays until a later item adds retirement.

| Shard | Typical content |
| --- | --- |
| Self | Who the companion is, how it reacts, general goals, self narratives, self model, capabilities, current feelings. |
| Entity (Jesse, a second companion, any other person) | That entity's narratives, interpersonal goals, plans, expectations, relationship state, shared memories. |
| Workflow (for example `minecraft`) | Locations, screenshots, plans, projects, task receipts, and world memories for that workflow. |

A second companion is an entity like a human user. The runtime does not tell them apart.

### Workspace items

`WorkspaceItem` holds `record_id`, `entered_tick`, `activation_at_entry`, `structure_id`, `reason`, `tokens`, and `required_by` (a requirement name or empty).
`WorkspaceShard` holds `key`, `items`, `tail_ids`, and the shard's requirement set name.
Items that enter a shard become causal. They enter the story that later prompts read.
Items that leave a shard stay causal for the time they were present.

### Turn record

`GwtTurnRecord` becomes a per-cycle change record.
For each shard it lists the entered items, the left items with reasons, the kept items, and the tail ids.
It also stores the broadcast summary and the subjective experience id.
Use a new schema id, `pm.gwt-turn.v2`. Old turns and old attention frames are not migrated. The new schema is the canon.
Delete `AttentionFrameRecord` from the live path. Attention decisions go into the turn record and `Structure` records.

## Current situational model

Port `CSMState`, `CSMItem`, and `CSMManager` from `private-machine-head/pm/csm/csm.py` into `pm/records/situational_model.py`.
Keep the class names close to the old ones, but follow the naming rules (`SituationalModel`, `SituationalItem`).

`SituationalItem` fields: `record_id`, `entered_tick`, `last_boost_tick`, `ticks_present`, `activation`, `base_activation`, `valence`, `salience`, `endorsers` (codelet names and other sources), `merged_ids`, and `shard_hint` (a `ShardKey` or none).

Operations:

- `decay_step()` multiplies activation by `CSM_DECAY` and adds one to `ticks_present`. Low base-level activation decays fast. High base-level activation decays slowly (LIDA).
- `add_or_boost(item, endorser)` merges an item into an existing item when their embeddings have cosine similarity of at least `CSM_MERGE_SIMILARITY`. A merge adds `CSM_BOOST` and records the endorser. Several codelets that pick the same content therefore raise its activation.
- `prune()` removes items below `CSM_MIN_ACTIVATION` and enforces `CSM_CAPACITY`. It never removes an item that a CSM requirement protects.
- `snapshot_for(query_embedding, codelet, attention_bias, max_tokens)` ports `GhostCodelets.get_snapshot`. It combines valence focus, salience sharpening, semantic relevance to the codelet signature, a pathway precursor bonus, and sampling noise. Each codelet sees its own view of the CSM.

Initial constants: `CSM_CAPACITY = 128`, `CSM_DECAY = 0.92`, `CSM_MERGE_SIMILARITY = 0.86`, `CSM_BOOST = 0.25`, `CSM_MIN_ACTIVATION = 0.1`.

CSM requirements use the same format as workspace requirements.
At least these items always stay in the CSM: the current stimuli, all narrative facets of the self, and all narrative facets of every entity in the current stimulus groups.

### What enters the CSM

The retrieval phase adds candidates with the endorser `retrieval`:

- current stimuli and the last dialogue turns of each group,
- episodic clusters, facts, and semantic records by relevance to each group,
- memories with a similar emotional state (port `_get_emotional_similary_memory`),
- all narrative records of the self and of the group entities,
- active goals, open expectations, and recent task receipts.

Codelets add percepts. Structure builders add structures.
The previous workspace items stay in the CSM with their activation.

## Structures

Use `Structure` from `pm/records/codelet_records.py`.
A structure is a bag of records with relations, affect, a theme, and a target shard.

Two structure builders run after the codelets:

1. `TopicalCoalitionBuilder` (pure Python) ports `_build_structures_get_coalitions`. It clusters CSM items by embedding (agglomerative, cosine, average linkage). It builds two coalition types, balanced by record type and hard by cluster.
2. `StructureBinder` (story-framed model call) reads the CSM snapshot and the recent broadcasts. It binds codelet percepts to memories and narratives with a stated reason. Example: "Spamuel should react angrily because X" binds to memory X' because the topic matches and the emotional state at that time was similar.

A structure can bind conflicting content. Cognitive dissonance is allowed and visible.
Each structure names its target shard. Attention can override that choice.

## Global workspace and shards

### Budgets

| Constant | Initial value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `WORKSPACE_SHARD_TOKENS` | 12000 | Content budget of each shard. |
| `WORKSPACE_TAIL_TOKENS` | 6000 | Extra budget of each shard for its tail. |
| `CCQ_LENGTH` | 8 | Number of recent broadcasts kept in the cycle state. |

The tail holds the newest stream of the shard:

- self shard: the latest internal thoughts and subjective experiences,
- entity shard: the latest dialogue with that entity,
- workflow shard: the latest results and receipts of that workflow.

Count tokens with `pm.llm.context_window.estimate_tokens` on the rendered story element of each record.

### Requirements

Requirements stop important content from being displaced and keep content balanced across types.
They are generic and live in `workflows/main_cognition/workspace.yaml`.

```yaml
shards:
  self:
    requirements:
      - name: self_narrative
        select: {record: Narrative, entity: self}
        min_items: 1
        max_items: 3
        max_token_share: 0.25
      - name: self_model
        select: {record: SemanticRecordKnoxel, category: self_model}
        min_items: 1
        max_token_share: 0.15
      - name: codelet_percepts
        select: {record: CodeletPercept}
        max_token_share: 0.35
  entity:
    requirements:
      - name: entity_narrative
        select: {record: Narrative, entity: shard}
        min_items: 1
        max_items: 2
        max_token_share: 0.2
      - name: memories
        select: {record: [MemoryClusterKnoxel, DeclarativeFactKnoxel]}
        max_token_share: 0.4
  workflow:
    requirements:
      - name: receipts
        select: {record: [ActionSchemaKnoxel, Feature], feature_type: WorldEvent}
        max_token_share: 0.4
```

`min_items` reserves a place that other content cannot take.
`max_items` and `max_token_share` are caps.
Attention decides which record fills a reserved place.
For example, if anger and introspection codelets dominate a cycle, a narrative facet that describes the companion as an angry jackass can replace the previous self narrative.
The exact selectors are part of the implementation. The final file must express the content types above.

### Selection

The attention phase updates each shard. It does not rebuild it.

1. Rate the structures for each shard (see Attention below).
2. Fill each `min_items` requirement with the best matching item from the winning structures. If no structure has one, use the best matching CSM item.
3. Add items from the winning structures in rating order until the budget or a cap stops them.
4. Keep an incumbent item while its activation, plus `WORKSPACE_HOLD_BONUS`, beats the best newcomer for its place.
5. Record every entered and left item with its tick and reason.

Incumbents give the workspace continuity. Decay and new content still replace them.

## Codelets

### Registry and activation

Port `CodeletSignature`, `CodeletRuntime`, `CodeletState`, `ActivationFactors`, and `CodeletRegistry` from `private-machine-head/pm/codelets/codelet.py`.
Store `CodeletRegistryState` in the cycle state. Every codelet has `activation`, `base_activation`, `cooldown_ticks`, `last_fired_tick`, and `total_runs`.

Activation combines:

- the stored activation,
- the stimulus match,
- a match between the mental state and the codelet families,
- cosine similarity between the codelet signature and the situation embedding,
- base-level activation,
- a recency penalty and a cooldown factor,
- a small random part that prevents deadlocks.

An LLM selector ports `AgentSelectCodelets`. It reads the shared story prefix and rates the top candidates for this moment. Its ratings add to activation.
Fix the old bug: iterate `.items()`.

Base-level activation rises when a percept from the codelet enters a workspace shard. It decays slowly in every cycle (LIDA attentional learning).

Codelets run in waves.
Each wave picks the highest-activation codelets up to the cycle's codelet budget.
Triage sets that budget for each cycle, from 1 for an unimportant input to 12 for a critical one.
Codelets in one wave run in parallel. A later wave sees the CSM after the earlier waves.

### Pathways

Keep `pathways_detailed.py` as data, unchanged.
When a codelet runs, find it in each pathway chain.
Boost the next registered codelet downstream by the step strength.
Multiply that strength by `PATHWAY_SKIP_DECAY` (initially 0.5) for each unregistered codelet skipped on the way.
Record the precursor percept ids, so the boosted codelet's CSM snapshot favors them (port `precursor_dict`).

### Execution modes

- `chain`: two calls, as in `AgentSimpleCodelet`. The first call writes the story recommendation as prose. The second converts it to typed percepts.
- `agent`: one pm-coder session with scoped memory tools, a CSM view tool, and the shared prefix. The `data_aquisation` list becomes the agent's retrieval plan. The session returns the same output model as a chain. Follow the `focus_management` pattern (`MemoryReadSession`, loopback MCP, cited ids checked against `memory.accessed`).

Codelets with the `MemoryAccessors` family default to `agent`.
More pm-coder sessions are allowed wherever they improve believability. Speed is not a constraint in this item (D16).

### Output

Each codelet returns `CodeletOutput` with `variants: list[CodeletVariant]`.
`CODELET_OUTPUT_VARIANTS = 1` sets the requested count. A later item can raise it and add selection agents for the most novel variants.

Each variant holds:

- `recommendation`: what the companion should feel, think, or do in this story, in the codelet's narrow concern,
- `percepts`: typed percepts sampled from the codelet families (port `codelet_percepts.py` and `sample_percept_types`),
- `target_shard`: the shard that the variant concerns.

Every percept carries `salience`, `valence`, `appraisal_general`, and `appraisal_social`.

### Mini-appraisal

For each percept, copy the current mental state, apply the percept appraisals, and call `compute_state_delta`.
Store the resulting appraisal and delta vectors on the `CodeletPercept` record.
Derive the percept's CSM activation from its valence, salience, and the attention bias (`compute_attention_bias`).
A percept that enters the workspace becomes causal. Its delta then enters the EMA mental state.
A percept that never enters stays unconscious and does not change the mental state.

Fix the old `model_for_percept` bug: the overrides dictionary was discarded.

### Prompt policy for codelets

Every codelet does exactly its own job, at full strength.

- Remove softeners from the copied prompts. Examples: "suggest a constructive redirection strategy", "avoiding overt aggression", "if it feels disruptive, generate a neutral observation instead".
- Tell each codelet to express its purpose maximally. It must not worry about being overly harsh, and it must not balance its view. Other codelets and attention supply the balance.
- Frame the output as a recommendation for the character in a creative story, never as advice to an assistant.
- Do not add capability or routing rules to codelet prompts.

## Prompt frame

All model calls in the cycle are unconscious processes.
None of them speaks as the companion to the user.
Each call is an immersive character development and choice simulator.

Every call has the same structure, so the provider can reuse the cached prefix:

1. A shared system prompt: an expert story writer who simulates the mind of a complex character (port the frame from `AgentSimpleCodelet` and `_deliberate_and_select_reply`).
2. A shared story prefix: the character card, then the self shard, then the shards of the current groups, each with its tail, rendered as story in world-time order (port `KnoxelList.get_story`).
3. The concrete instruction for this codelet, builder, or phase, last.

Shard order and record order are deterministic within one cycle.
The prompt contains no embeddings.
Capability limits and workflow rules appear only in the routing phase.

## Affect

Delete `project_affect` and `affect_dynamics.py`.
Appraisal and causal percepts become `Feature` records with appraisal and delta vectors.
The mental state is `ema_baselined_normalize` over the delta history of causal features (port `_compute_mental_state`).
Ego strength sets the reference time frame, as in `ghost_codelets.cognitive_cycle`.
The relationship state is computed per entity from that entity's features.

## Stimulus groups and actions

### Groups

Each stimulus belongs to exactly one group:

- a message or event from an entity goes to the group of that entity,
- a workflow result or event goes to the group of that workflow,
- an own thought or a system event goes to the self group.

Triage rates each group. Insignificant groups still enter the CSM, but get no decision.

### Decision

The decision phase runs once for each group that triage did not mark insignificant.
It reads the shared prefix and the group's stimuli.
It returns zero or one `ActionDecision` for the group, with variants (`DECISION_VARIANTS = 1`).
The decision is written as a recommendation for the character in the story.
Example: "He has dodged choosing four times. Call him a coward for it, then admit that you like the story unfinished too."
It contains no routing rules, no capability lists, and no workflow names.

### Routing

The routing phase is a separate, unconscious step.
It maps each decision to an action type, a workflow, arguments, and presentation.
It holds every capability rule, Minecraft rule, task rule, and presentation rule from the current action selection prompt.
It must keep the decision's intent and voice. It passes the decision text to the workflow unchanged.
A deterministic conflict check runs after routing. Two actions cannot claim the same body lease, and one group cannot get two actions.
Each dispatch carries its group, the group's stimulus ids, and the group's correlation id.

### Expectations

Each routed action records an expected reaction as an `ExpectationRecord` (port `_generate_expectations_for_action`).
Appraisal checks open expectations against new stimuli (port `_appraise_stimulus_check_expectation`).
Fix the old inverted similarity threshold.

## Deliberate focus

`focus_management` results become CSM endorsements with the endorser `deliberate_focus`.
They add at most `FOCUS_ACTIVATION_WEIGHT` to an item.
Deliberate focus can fill at most `FOCUS_MAX_SHARE` of a shard budget.
It cannot pin items and cannot remove items that requirements protect.
The companion can steer its mind, but cannot fully control it.
Initial values: `FOCUS_ACTIVATION_WEIGHT = 0.3`, `FOCUS_MAX_SHARE = 0.3`.

## Departures from the base references

Record these as deliberate engineering choices, not as LIDA parameters.

- Token budgets for shards and tails. LIDA only says that conscious content is small.
- Content requirements that always keep a self narrative and an entity narrative in the workspace. Kronsted describes narratives as mostly preconscious. A reserved place keeps the character stable for the language model.
- Shards for self, entities, and workflows. LIDA has one workspace.
- Zero or one action per group. LIDA Smooth Coping allows several non-conflicting behaviors in one selection. A19F uses groups and a conflict check in place of a behavior network.
- Codelet cooldown. It follows inhibition of return, not a LIDA rule.

Watch the self-narrative feedback loop (Kronsted).
Requirements keep a narrative present, but attention chooses the facet.
Contradicting evidence must still reach the workspace.

## Out of scope

- The inner loop on the shared cycle: [A19G](a19g.md).
