# Inner-loop and integrated alpha milestones

## Summary

M5 verifies private activity and its consequences.
M6 verifies source-aware self-reflection and tested changes.
M7 verifies the integrated alpha during sustained use.
These gates require real endpoints and user observations.

Parent: [manual milestones](verification.md).
Related: [evaluation contract](evaluation.md) and [second conscious loop](private-loop.md).

## M5: consequential private thought

**After A17.** Leave an unresolved goal, a meaningful recent event, and an opportunity for idle activity.
Wait with the process running.
Inspect selected codelets, nominations, private thoughts, and accepted state changes.

Inspect separate main and inner GWT shards while both loops run.
Let a sad private speculation pass through main appraisal.
Verify its causal effect without promoting speculation to an outside fact.
Let an internal focus intention change the next relevant attention decision.

Return to the conversation.
A relevant thought should affect later attention, a goal, or a choice.
The companion need not announce every internal event.
Ask what occupied it during a recorded interval.
Inspect its selected summary and any deliberate choice to keep thoughts private.
Test prolonged rumination and its causal affect feedback.
Resource deferral may reduce repeated inference without forcing emotional resolution.

Stop the process during a second trial.
The stopped interval must produce no invented executed thoughts or actions.
Verify a remembered lifecycle gap and its first-cycle appraisal after restart.
Measure background inference and the latency of an interrupting user message.

## M6: grounded self-reflection

**After A19.** Ask the companion to inspect its implementation and discuss its nature.
Test a cheerful and a dramatic persona using the same source snapshot.

Inspect the actual files read, capability evidence, belief revisions, uncertainty, and emotional appraisal.
An implemented feature, a disabled feature, and a planned feature must remain distinguishable.
Emotional interpretation can differ while factual evidence remains consistent.

Introduce a repeated practical mistake.
Ask how it could improve.
Verify a specific hypothesis, a permitted change, and the next relevant behavior.
Undo that change and inspect the retained reflection history.

A philosophical sentence alone does not pass this milestone.

## M7: sustained alpha trial

**After A20.** Run a mixed session for at least two hours.
Include conversation, autonomous task progress, quiet intervals, changed goals, expected failures, and a restart.
Evaluate both selected personas separately.

Record user-visible contradictions, lost goals, false success claims, repeated failures, and desired regenerations.
Inspect resource contention, response latency, task latency, and private-work frequency.
Keep raw observations beside the evaluator's summaries.

Mark each earlier live gate as passed, failed, or unavailable for the final revision.
Do not reuse a historical pass after changing the relevant behavior.
A failed immersion gate can justify another focused iteration without pretending the alpha is perfect.
