# A13: Replan expected gameplay failures

## Summary

This item treats expected gameplay failures as evidence for recovery or a changed method.
It is not implemented by this plan.

Parent: [ordered alpha plan](../index.md).
Dependencies: A11, A12.
Design: [outcomes and recovery](../minecraft-operations.md#outcomes-and-recovery).

### Deliver

1. Distinguish transport failure, unsuccessful action, partial progress, changed assumptions, and completion evidence.
2. Implement recovery priorities and replanning with accumulated method/search history.
3. Feed meaningful progress and failures into memory and cognition without flooding them with every call.

A game failure is expected evidence.
It is not always a reason to restart the actor or retry the same command.
A service outage uses a separate retained-state waiting path.

### Start from

The survival skill's no-progress, geometry, hazard, mining, and recovery rules.
The older `pm_next` actor/replanner outcome contracts.
Current managed receipts, consolidation, and A11 task specifications.

Record local search coverage, method, world/dimension, observation time, and uncertainty.
A failed local search cannot justify a universal resource-absence claim.
Retain successful work and changed preconditions.

### Verify

Use real fixture scenarios for blocked navigation, unreachable block, wrong harvesting tool, low supplies, water/lava danger, and unsuccessful ore search.
After two ineffective repetitions, verify a relevant diagnosis or method change.
Recovery must remain active until its evidence proves the hazard ended.

A replanner must add, revise, or defer a concrete leaf.
It must not only produce a better-sounding explanation.
Verify that cumulative partial progress survives a revised plan.

### Manual test

Obstruct a route and remove an assumed resource during work.
Observe adaptation.
Introduce a hazard and inspect its priority over the original goal.
Complete M3: a usable small house and an iron pickaxe from mined, smelted iron.
Verify that village supplies did not provide the iron.

### Handoff

Supply outcome taxonomy, no-progress rules, recovery tests, search-memory schema, and cognition event examples.
