# A07: Make emotional state change behavior

## Summary

This item connects affect dynamics to later attention, goals, regulation, and speech.
It is not implemented by this plan.

Parent: [ordered alpha plan](../index.md).
Dependencies: A06.
Design: [emotional dynamics](../cognition.md#emotional-dynamics).

### Deliver

1. Connect personality, needs, appraisal, expectation error, and elapsed time to bounded affect dynamics.
2. Use accepted affect to influence attention, goals, regulation, and expressive context.
3. Record causal traces and provide contrasting persona scenarios.

D08 confirms prolonged dark moods and rumination as core persona behavior.
D11 confirms startup/shutdown and offline-gap events for appraisal.
Support mixed feelings and persistence across topic changes.
Do not reset the persona to neutral after every reply.
Do not require coping to succeed or produce optimism.

### Start from

Current `pm_core/model/` mental-state and appraisal mathematics.
Compare `C:\source\pm\pm_next\pm_loop_engine\agentic\appraisal.py`.
Port useful pure calculations rather than its silent fallback behavior.

Keep configuration values inspectable and versioned.
An emotion label needs a defined behavioral effect.
Game danger can preempt slow regulation or contemplation.

### Verify

Run event sequences covering anticipation, success, disappointment, conflict, repair, and delayed reassurance.
Verify different plausible reactions from two trait profiles using the same facts.
Verify accumulation, decay, bounds, mixed state, and restart continuity.

Trace at least one change from event to appraisal, affect, attention, and later choice.
A negative hypothetical event may affect mood without becoming an observed fact.
A service failure must not masquerade as a neutral emotional response.

### Manual test

Introduce a disappointment, change the subject, and return to it later.
Offer repair and observe the rate and manner of recovery.
Inspect the internal cause alongside the public behavior.

### Handoff

Supply state equations, persona parameters, time semantics, behavioral mappings, and real scenario evidence.
List dimensions that exist in models but remain inactive.
